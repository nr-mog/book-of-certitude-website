# Retired pages

The Glossary and People pages, and the generators that built them, were retired
on 9 September 2026. Their job is now done inside the text itself: glossary
terms are marked in the passages on the Read and week pages and open in the
same popover as a footnote, using the published Kitáb-i-Íqán's own "Glossary
and Notes" (see `tools/gen_glossary_data.py`).

Quarto ignores directories whose names begin with an underscore, so nothing
here is rendered or published. Kept for reference — the biographies on the
People page were written for this study and have no equivalent in the
published glossary.
